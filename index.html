Frequently Asked Questions

          
Everything you need to know about disposable temporary emails.

        


        

          

            

              What is a temporary disposable email address?
              
            

            

              
A temporary email (also known as throwaway email, 10-minute mail, or burner email) allows you to receive emails at a temporary address that self-destructs after a set time. It protects your real inbox from spam, unwanted promos, tracking, and identity theft.

            

          


          

            

              Is MyTempMails completely free to use?
              
            

            

              
Yes! MyTempMails is 100% free with no hidden fees, subscriptions, or credit card requirements. You can create as many addresses as you need.

            

          


          

            

              Can I choose my own custom email username?
              
            

            

              
Yes. Click the Custom Handle button above to specify your preferred username and create a customized disposable inbox instantly.

            

          


          

            

              Can I receive file attachments in emails?
              
            

            

              
Yes, incoming messages with attachments (PDFs, images, documents) are fully supported and can be downloaded securely right from the email reader.

            

          


          

            

              Can I extend the 10-minute lifespan?
              
            

            

              
Absolutely. Click the +10 Min button next to the countdown timer anytime to extend the duration of your inbox for as long as you need.

            

          


          

            

              How does MyTempMails protect my online privacy?
              
            

            

              
MyTempMails does not require any personal info, passwords, or registration. All messages and temporary mailboxes are automatically purged, preventing data brokers and trackers from linking activities to your real identity.
